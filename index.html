12. Professional system report

Engineering safety note

Preliminary design only. This software does not replace a site survey, manufacturer instructions or professional electrical design. Battery efficiency and DoD are preliminary standard assumptions and should be replaced by manufacturer datasheet values when available. Verify real appliance nameplates, solar resource, temperature correction, battery charge/discharge limits, inverter surge capability, MPPT voltage/current limits, cable ampacity and voltage drop, overcurrent protection, DC isolators, earthing, lightning/surge protection and applicable local standards before installation.
